Check an RS-485 link

  • Confirm 120 ohm termination at the two physical ends only (about 60 ohm across A and B with power off).
  • Confirm bias at one point only, with 200 mV or more on the idle line.
  • Match baud rate, parity and stop bits on every device.
  • Swap A and B at one device if the line shows no activity.
  • Check the shield is grounded at one end only.

Check an Ethernet or fibre link

  • Look at the link and activity LEDs on the RTU port and the switch port.
  • Check for a speed or duplex mismatch on both ends.
  • On fibre, clean the connectors and swap Tx and Rx if no light is received.
  • Check the switch port is in the telemetry VLAN and not shut down.
  • Ping the RTU from a laptop on the same VLAN and check the IP address.
